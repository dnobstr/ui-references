# UI References Studio Workspace

## Scope and Mode

- **Primary target:** `src/app/page.tsx`
- **Visitor mode:** `operate`

## Audience and Task

Frontend engineers and design engineers capturing UI screenshots via clipboard paste (`Ctrl+V`/`Cmd+V`), file drop/upload, or direct URL, dissecting them with multi-provider vision AI (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude) into structured replication prompts, and saving both image and prompt into the repository's browsable reference archive.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An orthophoto survey quadrangle workbench that frames every UI screenshot as a surveyed plate with coordinate neatline ticks, extracted surface tokens, and a structured replication dossier, refusing the dark-neon AI SaaS dashboard default.
OWN-WORLD: Map-vellum ivory ground (`#F5F3EC`), deep survey slate (`#1E262B`), topographic copper-orange (`#C25934`), and hydrographic teal (`#246A73`), with hairline coordinate neatlines, capsule state controls with live indicator chips, and tabular coordinate readouts.
STORY: The user stages a screenshot in one action (paste, drop, or URL), inspects its dimensions and color palette inside the survey neatline, generates or refines the AI replication prompt, and commits the specimen to the repository catalog.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top survey command bar (`#1E262B`) with capsule ingestion status and inline AI provider configuration; split workbench below pairing the coordinate-framed screenshot stage and 3-way ingestion bar on the left with the structured AI prompt dossier and primary "Generate Prompt" / "Save to Repo" actions on the right, above the registered 16:10 repository catalog grid.
FORM: Orthophoto Survey Quadrangle (Swisstopo / USGS Survey Sheet), position 6 of 7, seed:506948a2bcb147b5.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
