# UI References Studio

**Turn any UI screenshot into a ready-to-use design prompt.** Browse curated interface references or drop in your own screenshots to extract structured layouts, color palettes, typography, and CSS tokens for your favorite AI coding and design tools.

---

## What You Can Do

- **Search & Browse References** — Explore the built-in catalog by keyword, layout style, or tag (`Dark UI`, `Landing Page`, `Dashboard`, etc.) to inspect real UI breakdowns and copy proven prompts.
- **Turn Any Screenshot into a Prompt** — Paste an image straight from your clipboard (`Ctrl+V` / `Cmd+V`), drag and drop a file, or paste an image URL.
- **One-Click Copy for AI Builders** — Copy the **Master Prompt**, **Layout Anatomy**, **Typography**, **CSS Tokens**, or **Interactions** directly into Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, v0, Lovable, or any AI assistant.
- **Save Your Own Library** — Keep your favorite screenshots and generated prompts organized in your personal collection.

---

## How It Works

1. **Explore or Add a Screenshot**
   Search the existing reference catalog, or paste/drop any UI screenshot into the studio.
2. **Choose Your Analysis Mode**
   - **Built-in Analyzer (Default):** Works instantly with no account or API key required.
   - **Bring Your Own AI Key (Optional):** Open **Provider Settings** in the app to connect **Google Gemini**, **OpenAI**, or **Anthropic Claude** for deeper AI vision breakdowns.
3. **Copy & Build**
   Switch between the 5 specification tabs, edit any details if you'd like, and click **Copy** to paste the prompt into your AI tool of choice.

---

## Privacy & API Keys

Your API keys and personal saved references stay in your own browser (`localStorage`) and are never shared with other users.
